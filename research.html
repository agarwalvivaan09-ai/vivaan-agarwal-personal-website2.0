QUANTITATIVE FINANCE · FINANCIAL RESILIENCE

Household Financial Resilience Index

Independent Research · 2026

The Household Financial Resilience Index (HFRI) is a quantitative framework designed to measure a household's capacity to absorb financial shocks while continuing to meet essential obligations.

The index combines four dimensions of household financial health: savings strength, debt sustainability, emergency liquidity and expenditure stability. Each dimension is transformed into a transparent scoring function and combined into a composite index ranging from 0 to 100.

The research develops and calibrates the mathematical functions, tests the index using simulated household data, examines its sensitivity to alternative weights, and compares its behaviour against an external financial-resilience benchmark.

Financial modelling · Quantitative analysis · Index construction · Risk & resilience · Household finance
